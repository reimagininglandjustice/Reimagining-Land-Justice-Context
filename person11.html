John R.F. Lewis (1983-2021)

The late John R.F. Lewis, co-founder of Renew Richmond, was a transformational leader in Richmond’s Black food justice movement. A passionate advocate for nutrition, sustainability, and food security, Lewis worked tirelessly to engage inner-city youth in both Roanoke and Richmond, using gardening and urban agriculture as tools for education, empowerment, and healing.

His efforts helped lay the foundation for Richmond’s urban agriculture policy and inspired a generation of activists and community builders.

John’s work was rooted in justice and love for his community. Through every garden planted and every young person mentored, he helped grow not only food—but also hope, agency, and resilience. His vision lives on in the thriving food justice movement he helped cultivate.
