Malcom X (1925-1965)

A powerful and transformative leader, Malcolm X was a vocal advocate for Black self-determination, human rights, and resistance to racial oppression. His views on land and economic independence evolved throughout his life, from his early advocacy for Black separatism to his later calls for global solidarity among oppressed peoples.

Malcolm X believed that land was central to true freedom and economic autonomy for Black people. He argued that Black Americans should reclaim control over their own land and resources, both as a way to challenge white supremacy and as a means of building strong, self-sustaining communities.

His work continues to inspire movements for land justice, economic empowerment, and decolonization.
