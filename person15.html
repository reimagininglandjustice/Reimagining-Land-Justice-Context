Dara Cooper (b.1978)

Food Justice Activista. Writer. Organizer. Abolitionist.

Dara Cooper is a dynamic activist and organizer with roots in Chicago, Atlanta, and Jackson, MS. She is the co-founder and former executive director of the National Black Food and Justice Alliance (NBFJA), where she currently serves as a strategic advisor. NBFJA unites Black-led organizations to advance visions for just, sustainable communities and build institutions supporting Black food and land sovereignty.

Cooper has played pivotal roles in the HEAL Food Alliance, the Movement for Black Lives policy table, and the Kataly Foundation’s Environmental Justice Resource Collective, distributing millions to BIPOC environmental justice groups nationwide. She also advised Solidaire’s Black Liberation Pooled Fund.

A contributor to Bryant Terry’s Black Food book and co-author of scholarly work on Black feminist food justice, Dara’s writing and organizing continue to inspire transformative change toward food sovereignty and liberation.
