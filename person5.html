Regina Brave (1941- )

An Oglala Lakota activist and land defender, Regina Brave has been a lifelong advocate for Indigenous sovereignty, environmental protection, and treaty rights. She played a key role in the 1973 Wounded Knee occupation and was a prominent voice during the 2016 Standing Rock protests against the Dakota Access Pipeline.

Her activism centers on the sacred relationship between Indigenous peoples and the land, emphasizing that protecting water, earth, and future generations is both a spiritual and political duty. She speaks out against colonial land grabs, environmental destruction, and broken promises, urging unity and resistance in defense of Indigenous rights.

Her leadership continues to inspire movements for land justice rooted in Indigenous knowledge and resilience.
