Richmond Grows Gardens

Richmond City's Community Garden Program

Richmond Grows Gardens is a city-supported initiative that transforms vacant, city-owned land into vibrant community gardens. The program supports neighborhood-based agriculture to improve quality of life, promote environmental resilience, and expand access to fresh, healthy food, especially in under-resourced areas.

Through an open application process, the program leases land to community groups and organizations to cultivate fruits, vegetables, herbs, and flowers. These gardens not only feed neighborhoods but also foster community connection and educational opportunities around food and sustainability.

Happily Natural manages three active gardens through Richmond Grows Gardens, helping to cultivate green spaces that grow both food and community across the city.
