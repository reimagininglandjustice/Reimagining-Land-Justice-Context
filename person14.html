Victoria Campbell (1954-2024)

Victoria was an artist, gardener, activist, and volunteer dedicated to making Richmond a healthier, greener, and more vibrant city. As the Community Gardens Coordinator for The City of Richmond, Vicki managed 14 community gardens through the Richmond Grows Gardens program, helping residents create organic gardens on city properties and fostering spaces where people could connect with nature and each other.

Vicki took pride in her work to ensure everyone had access to healthy food and beautiful outdoor places to enjoy. Beyond gardening, she contributed to city beautification projects, managed volunteers and community service workers, and conducted historical and infrastructure research to support park improvements.
