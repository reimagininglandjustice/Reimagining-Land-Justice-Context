Arthur "Art" Burton (b.1956)

Arthur “Art” Burton is a community political activist, orator, and entrepreneur. Burton is known for his transformative work in public housing neighborhoods. Born in Youngstown, Ohio, and raised in Richmond, Art has dedicated his life to uplifting marginalized communities through grassroots initiatives rooted in empowerment and justice.

In 2011, Art founded Kinfolk Community Empowerment Center, a nonprofit organization based in the Mosby Court housing project in Richmond’s East End. Kinfolk serves as a hub for community development, offering programs that address food insecurity, youth engagement, and economic opportunity.

Art’s work is deeply influenced by his father, who was a community activist in Richmond during the 1970s. Continuing his father's legacy, Art is committed to dismantling systemic barriers and creating opportunities for Black and low-income communities to thrive.
